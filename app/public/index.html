Privacy & Data

Shelter Walk has no report, export, or admin view anywhere that lists who walked which dog, or that compares volunteers to each other. Combined stats are always anonymous, and shared tips are never signed. Once a walk is over, who did it is never shown to anyone - including staff - no matter what you choose below.

Available shows "Being walked by a Volunteer" instead of your name. This is the only place your name ever appears while walking; it only matters while the walk is still in progress.
