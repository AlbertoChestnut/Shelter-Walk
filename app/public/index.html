Privacy & Data

Shelter Walk has no report, export, or admin view anywhere that lists who spent time with which animal, or that compares volunteers to each other. Combined stats are always anonymous, and shared tips are never signed. Once it's over, who did it is never shown to anyone - including staff - no matter what you choose below.

Available shows "a Volunteer" instead of your name on the "currently out" badge. This is the only place your name ever appears; it only matters while you're still out with them.

Feedback & bug reports

Found a bug, something confusing, or have an idea? Every report is read. Reports are anonymous: your name and account are never saved with them, so if you'd like a reply, say how to reach you.
