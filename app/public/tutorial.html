How to use Shelter Walk

Shelter Walk shows which dogs need a walk, lets you check a dog out by scanning the QR code or barcode on their kennel card, and times the walk. The most important part is scanning a dog for a walk.

Getting started

  1. Sign in with the invite link from the volunteer coordinator.
  2. Add it to your Home Screen. On iPhone, use Safari's Share button, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, use Chrome's menu, then Install app. Walk alerts on iPhone only work from the Home Screen app.
  3. Enter your name. Other volunteers see it only while you are out with a dog.
  4. Pick your experience level. It decides which dogs show as ready for you. If you're not sure, choose Beginner. You can change it later in Settings.

Scanning a dog for a walk

Tap Scan, point your phone at the QR code or barcode on the kennel card, check the dog's screen, then tap Start Walk. The app reads the code by itself, so there's nothing to press.

1. Open the camera

Tap 📷 Scan in the bottom bar. The camera opens right away. The first time, your phone asks for camera access, so tap Allow.

2. Scan the QR code or the barcode on the kennel card

Every kennel card has two codes, and either one works. Both hold the dog's Animal ID, so they open the same dog. Use whichever is easier to reach or less scuffed.

  • The QR code is the small square near the top right, next to the dog's photo.
  • The barcode is the set of vertical lines under the breed and colors.

Fit the code inside the green corners and hold still for a second. The app reads it by itself. Move closer if the code looks small, and tilt the phone if the plastic sleeve shows glare. For the barcode, fit every line inside the corners, edge to edge.

3. Check it's the right dog

Match the name and ID number to the kennel card. Axel Rose's card says Animal ID: 262826, and so does the app. Then read the badge under the name:

  • Green Eligible to walk ✓: you're cleared for this dog.
  • ⚠ Warning on a striped background: you're not cleared for this dog at

your level. The badge says why, such as "Experienced Volunteers Only" or "Needs 4 more days in shelter". Pick a different dog.

  • Solid red with 🚫 Too young to walk: puppies can't go out. The button is locked.
  • Yellow Potty Break Only banner: a short, potty-focused walk only.

4. Read the tips

Shared tips from other walkers and your own private notes are right under the dog. Tap a sticker to see what it means.

5. Fix anything that's changed, then start

If the kennel card's stickers or kennel letter differ from the app, tap them to match. Save Changes appears only when something changed, and Start Walk saves it too.

Tap Start Walk. If you're cuddling, at matchmaking, at play group, or taking the dog on a 🚗 daycation instead, tap ⇄ Change first and pick that. A daycation can last up to 12 hours, so it doesn't stop after 30 minutes the way a walk does.

6. Write the check-out time on the kennel

The big time at the top is your check-out time. The screen also shows Return to: with the kennel letter and a running timer.

A walk stops by itself after 30 minutes. Tap ＋10 min to extend it, up to 3 hours. If the limit stops a walk, the app asks when it really ended next time you open it.

7. End the walk

Bring the dog back and tap End Walk. The walk ends when you tap it, not when you save. Add an optional note for other walkers (shared without your name) and a private note, then tap Save & Finish. Scan Next Dog takes you straight back to the camera.

If the code won't scan

Type the dog's name in Search by name instead under the camera. Tap the dog, and you land on the same screen as a scan.

Scanned the wrong dog? Tap Scan a different dog, or your phone's Back button, to return to the camera. Nothing starts until you tap Start Walk.

The other screens

Four tabs run along the bottom: Available, Scan, Stats and Updates. Three buttons sit at the top right: 👤 your account, 📖 the Guide, and ⚙ Settings.

🐾 Available

Lists every dog, with stickers, days in the shelter, kennel letter, walks this week and an Eligible badge. Tap ▶ Walk / Edit to open a dog the same way a scan does, View Info for their profile, or 🕘 for walk history. Tick Out if a dog was already walked today outside the app.

Tap Filters to narrow the list by kennel wing, sex, age, breed, stickers, "Dogs I've walked before", or dogs still needing a walk this shift. Save a combination under Presets.

📊 Stats

My stats shows your walks today and over your lifetime. Tap ✎ to fix a walk's times or 🗑 to delete it. Together shows anonymous totals for the whole shelter, with no names and no rankings.

📣 Updates, 📖 Guide, ⚙ Settings, 👤 My Account

  • Updates: dogs that arrived, came back, or were adopted. Choose which

kinds show in Settings.

  • Guide: what every sticker means, tips for new volunteers, experience

levels, and shelter contacts.

  • Settings: experience level, usual activity, light or dark theme,

notifications, and walk-length alerts (up to 3, e.g. 7 and 10 minutes).

  • My Account: change your name, hide your name while walking, download or

delete your data, and sign out.

Troubleshooting

ProblemFix
"Camera access is blocked"Android: open Chrome, then Menu, Settings, Site settings, and search "shelterwalk.com". Set Camera to Allow, then reopen the app. iPhone: open the Settings app, then Safari, then Camera. Set it to Ask or Allow, then reopen the app. Until then, use Search by name.
The camera shows the code but nothing happensHold the phone 6–12 inches away, keep the whole code inside the green corners, and avoid glare. If one code is damaged, scan the other one: QR code or barcode. If both are, search by name.
"Could not read a dog ID from that code"You scanned something other than a kennel tag. Scan the dog's kennel card, or search by name.
"New kennel card · We don't have this dog's details yet"The shelter printed the card before adding the dog to its online listing. Pick the kennel letter and tap Start Walk as usual, after checking the card's age and stickers yourself. The walk is kept under the card's Animal ID and added to the dog's record once the listing has them (it's checked every 30 minutes).
The dog's screen shows the wrong dogTap Scan a different dog and scan again. Tell staff if a kennel card has the wrong dog's code.
You forgot to end a walkIt stops by itself at the time limit. Next time you open the app, choose when it really ended: Just now, At the limit, or Pick a time.
A finished walk has the wrong timesGo to Stats, find the walk, tap ✎ and fix it.
No walk alerts on iPhoneAdd the app to your Home Screen, open it from there, and turn alerts on in Settings.
